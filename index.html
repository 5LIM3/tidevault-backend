Ship's log

Three legs of the voyage

No trading, no timing the market. Anchor your tokens, wait out the term, collect your haul.
Leg One — Depart

Drop anchor

Sign up, then lock any amount of $DRIFT (min $500 equivalent) for a term of your choosing — 30, 50, 70, or 100 days.

Leg Two — Hold Course

Weather the term

Your tokens stay anchored until the term ends, but your share of the Tide Pool accrues continuously from day one.

Leg Three — Make Port

Collect your haul

Claim accrued rewards any time from your dashboard, without waiting for your term to end.

How does it work?

Lock your $DRIFT to receive a share of the Tide Pool

  • Fees collected across the DriftPort ecosystem flow into the Tide Pool.
  • More $DRIFT anchored = a larger share of the Tide Pool.
  • A term multiplier is applied to your share of the pool based on how long you lock.
